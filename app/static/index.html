Why does UniProt give me several records for this protein?

Type a protein or gene. We show every matching UniProt record, what differs, and where each claim comes from. We do not pick a “best” record for you.

Discovery mode

This is separate from direct protein/gene identity matching. A result appears only when one UniProt-exposed annotation, linked database field, or linked literature title contains traceable evidence for your wording.
